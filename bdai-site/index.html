Introducing

Mathopea

A mathematics organization built to make people fall in love with how math looks, thinks, and works.

The problem

Most people meet math as a wall of numbers, not as a designed idea.

Textbooks explain math. Almost nothing shows it: the patterns, symmetry, and structure that make it beautiful in the first place.

Introducing

Mathopea turns mathematics into a visual, hands-on organization.

See it

Every concept gets a visual form: geometry, motion, and pattern before formula.

Build it

Learners construct ideas themselves through structured, guided challenges.

Prove it

Every module ends with a real check for understanding, not just completion.

How it works

Three steps, every module.

01

Visualize

An interactive diagram introduces the concept before any notation appears.

02

Manipulate

Learners drag, resize, and rebuild the idea until the pattern clicks.

03

Explain

A short quiz confirms the idea transferred, not just that a screen was seen.

Design philosophy

Math already has a design system. We just put it on screen.

Grids, symmetry, ratio, and proof: the same structures mathematicians use to think are the structures Mathopea uses to teach.

Senior Secondary Mathematics

Three papers of 50 marks each, 150 marks in all. Each paper is 2 hours and uses the attached formula sheet. Open any page to read it full size.

Source: Question Paper and Answer Key, Parts I–III

General instructions

  1. All questions are compulsory. Internal choices are marked OR; attempt only one of them.
  2. Section A: MCQ, 1 mark each. Section B: Very Short Answer, 2 marks each. Section C: Short Answer, 3 marks each. Section D: Case-Based, 4 marks each. Section E: Long Answer, 5 or 6 marks each.
  3. Show all working for Sections B to E. Use of calculators is not permitted.
  4. The attached formula sheet may be used in all three papers. Each paper is of 2 hours.
