// The company

One company. Three engines of technology.

Databell started as a four-person data consultancy. Today it is a technology group that designs software, modernises the systems companies already have, and trains the people who keep them running.

  • Engineering first

    Every engagement is led by engineers who write production code, not slide decks.

  • Secure by default

    Zero-trust architecture, audited pipelines and ISO 27001-aligned delivery.

  • Knowledge that stays

    We hand over documentation, training and runbooks so your team owns the result.

2014

Databell founded

Data analytics consultancy, four engineers, one shared office.

2017

DatabellSoftwareLabs

Product engineering division launches; first SaaS platform ships.

2020

TechEnhance

Modernisation practice formed to move legacy systems to the cloud.

2022

TechKnowledge

Learning arm opens with 12 courses and a public knowledge base.

2026

AI-native group

All three divisions run on a shared AI platform across 9 countries.

          // FAQ
          
    Questions, answered.
    
          
    Can't find what you need? Our team replies within one business day.
    
          Ask us directly 
        
    
        
    
          
    
            Can I work with just one Databell extension?
            
    Yes. DatabellSoftwareLabs, TechEnhance and TechKnowledge each take engagements on their own. Many clients start with one and add the others later.
    
          
    
          
    
            How quickly can a Labs squad start?
            
    Usually within two weeks of signing. Discovery starts in week one and you see working software by the end of the first sprint.
    
          
    
          
    
            Will modernisation take our systems offline?
            
    No. TechEnhance migrates piece by piece behind feature flags and traffic shadowing, so the old system keeps serving until the new one is proven.
    
          
    
          
    
            Are TechKnowledge certificates recognised?
            
    Each certificate has a public verification link, and the cloud tracks map to the official AWS, Azure and GCP exam objectives.
    
          
    
          
    
            Who owns the code you write?
            
    You do. All source code, infrastructure definitions and documentation are transferred to you, with no lock-in.
