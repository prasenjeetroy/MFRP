The small model wakes up in the morning and reads a little book.
The little book has short sentences about the sun, the moon, and the sea.
The sun is warm. The moon is quiet. The sea is deep and wide.
A small dog runs across the green field, chasing a red ball.
The dog barks once, then twice, then runs back home for water.
In the kitchen, a cat sleeps on a warm chair near the window.
Outside, the wind moves the leaves of the old oak tree.
Rain falls softly on the roof, and the room feels calm and safe.
A child draws a picture of a house, a tree, and a bright yellow sun.
The picture is not perfect, but it is full of color and joy.
At night, the stars come out one by one above the quiet town.
People sleep, and the small model keeps reading its little book.
Tomorrow the sun will rise again, and the story will continue.
The small model learns one letter at a time, then one word at a time.
Soon it can guess what comes next in a simple, familiar sentence.
It is not a large model, but it tries its best with what it has.
Practice makes the small model a little better every single day.
