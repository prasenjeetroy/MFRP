        AI Playground

        Teach the robot to sort fruit from vegetables.

        The robot has never seen food before. Show it examples: how sweet is it, how crunchy is it? It colours the whole map with its best guess by asking the nearest neighbours you gave it. That is how a real machine-learning model called k-NN works.
