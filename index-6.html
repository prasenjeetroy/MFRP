    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    What is the PIN security key?

    A 4- or 6-digit PIN that quickly unlocks your account on this device, auto-locks it after inactivity and authorizes subscription payments and other sensitive changes. During setup you also get a one-time recovery security key to reset a forgotten PIN.

    Do I need a password?

    No. BDAI is password-free. When you sign up with your first and last name we create your personal address — like firstname.lastname@wellness.inbox.bdai — and you sign in with that address plus your PIN security key.

    What is my @wellness.inbox.bdai inbox?

    Every account gets its own BDAI inbox. Welcome notes, sign-in and security alerts, subscription receipts and coach messages are delivered there — open it from the inbox icon in the header or the Inbox tab in your account.

    What happens if I enter the wrong PIN too many times?

    Every 5 incorrect attempts trigger a cooldown that doubles each time (30s, 60s, 120s…). After 15 incorrect attempts your account is frozen and can only be unlocked with your recovery security key. Each alert is also sent to your BDAI inbox.

    How does the BDAI Wallet work?

    You get a welcome bonus in BDAI Cash when you sign up. Log workouts, finish daily tasks and reach milestones to earn more cash plus Wellness Points. Subscriptions are paid from your wallet balance, and points can be redeemed at checkout for up to 50% off. Renewals are paid from your wallet automatically.

    What do I need to buy a subscription?

    Only your PIN and your recovery security key. No card details are needed — the plan is paid from your BDAI Wallet, and both keys are required so nobody can spend your balance with just your PIN.

    I lost my security key — what now?

    Choose “Lost your security key?” at checkout or in Account → Security. We send a 6-digit code to your BDAI inbox, which only opens with your PIN. Copy the code, paste it in, and you get a brand-new security key. Codes expire after 10 minutes.

    Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

    Yes. Cancel from Account → Subscription. You keep access until the end of your current billing period, and you can resume before then with one click.

    Is my data secure?

    There are no passwords to leak. PINs and recovery keys are never stored in plain text — they're salted and hashed with PBKDF2-SHA-256. No card details are ever collected — purchases use your BDAI Wallet and need both your PIN and security key.

    Who is BDAIWellness?

    BDAIWellness is the wellness-intelligence engine that powers BDAI Sports & Health — the models behind readiness scores, adaptive plans and nutrition targets.
